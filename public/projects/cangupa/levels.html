Levels

Ten packs, 50 levels each

The packs form one collection, Lay of the Land. Each is named after a piece of country and played on a slightly bigger board than the one before.

  • Pack 1 · 3×3 · Common tiles

    Meadow

    Open grass everywhere underfoot. Walk the whole meadow without leaving a blade untouched.

  • Pack 2 · 4×3 · Adds gold tiles

    Creek

    A streambed of golden stones, slick till you've stood on them twice, and one washed clean through.

  • Pack 3 · 4×4 · Adds steel tiles

    Gully

    Steel grey scree that only lets go after three footfalls.

  • Pack 4 · 5×4 · Gold and steel

    Outcrop

    Weathered rock, part gold veined, part steel grey, exposed side by side.

  • Pack 5 · 5×5 · Adds departure tiles

    Billabong

    Still water that only drains one way, however it pooled in.

  • Pack 6 · 6×5 · Gold, steel and departure

    Ridge

    A spine of rock that turns from gold to steel grey as you walk it, always leading on in a single direction.

  • Pack 7 · 6×6 · Adds arrival tiles

    Mesa

    Sheer on every side but one; the only way up is the one true face.

  • Pack 8 · 7×6 · Gold, steel and arrival

    Gorge

    Narrow walls shifting from gold to steel grey as you descend, with just one way through at each turn.

  • Pack 9 · 7×7 · Gold, steel and arrival

    Plateau

    Tableland where gold ground, steel ground and narrow one way crossings all meet.

  • Pack 10 · 8×7 · Every tile kind

    Outback

    Wild country holding a bit of everything the game has crossed, together at last, on its widest stretch.
