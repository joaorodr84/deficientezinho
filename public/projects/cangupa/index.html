Cangupa

Hop on every tile. Leave none behind.

A kangaroo, a board of tiles and one rule: land on every tile before you run out of moves. 500 levels, from a 3×3 meadow to the wide outback.

How it works

Every tile you leave is gone

Step one tile, or jump two in a straight line. The tile you were standing on drops away behind you, so each move closes off part of the board. Clear the level by visiting every tile, which means finding a route that never strands you.

The two moves, the five kinds of tile, and what the buttons under the board do.

Features →

Hints from a real solver, stats, achievements, two languages and light and dark themes.

Levels →

Ten packs of 50 levels, each on a bigger board and with a new twist.

Where it comes from →

Cangupa is inspired by WESP, a DOS game about jumping through space.
