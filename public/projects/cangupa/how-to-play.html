How to play

Step on every tile to clear the level

You start on the tile marked with the kangaroo. Tap a highlighted tile to move there.

  • 1StepMove one tile up, down, left or right.
  • 2JumpMove two tiles in a straight line, over a gap or over another tile. The tile you jump over is left untouched.
  • 3Tiles drop awayYou can only land on a tile that still has visits left. A tile disappears as soon as you leave it for the last time.
  • 4Dead end?If no move is left, undo a few moves or restart the level.
Tiles

Five kinds of tile

Each pack of levels introduces a kind of tile or combines the ones you already know.

  • Common tilesOne visit, then gone.
  • Gold tilesNeed two visits. The strokes on a tile show how many visits it has left.
  • Steel tilesNeed three visits.
  • Departure tilesCan only be left through the edge the bracket opens on.
  • Arrival tilesCan only be entered through the edge the bracket opens on.
Buttons

Under the board

  • HintHighlights the solver's suggested next move. Each save starts with three hints.
  • UndoTakes back your last move.
  • RestartResets the level to its starting position.
  • HelpOpens the rules in a popup without leaving the level.
Where it comes from

Inspired by WESP

WESP (Weltraumspringen, "space jumping") is a DOS game with the same idea at its heart: hop across a field of tiles and use every one. Cangupa began as a plain HTML and JavaScript take on it and grew its own tiles, level packs and kangaroo from there.
