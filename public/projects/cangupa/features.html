Features

What the app does today

  • 500 levels in ten packsBoards grow from 3×3 to 8×7, and each pack brings in a new kind of tile or mixes the earlier ones.
  • Every level can be solvedLevels are generated as the trail of a walk of legal moves, so a solution always exists. Many candidates are tried for each level and the one with the fewest solutions is kept.
  • Hints from a real solverA hint is the solver's next move from where you stand, so it always still leads to a win.
  • Undo and restartStep back one move at a time, or reset the level, as often as you like.
  • StatsLevels cleared, total moves, hints and undos used, time played and your longest streak, with a breakdown per pack.
  • Twelve achievementsFor clearing levels and packs, for clean clears without a hint or an undo, for streaks of them, and for moves made.
  • English and PortuguesePick a language, or follow the device's own.
  • Light and dark themesChoose in Settings. Try it with the switch in the top right of this page.
  • Your way to moveTap a tile, or turn on a directional pad under the board. Possible moves can be highlighted, and animations and sound can be switched off.
  • Screen reader supportEvery tile on the board is described: its kind, its position and how many visits it has left.
