Cangupa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Cangupa is a puzzle game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, what it doesn't, and how the services it relies on handle data.

Information We Collect

Cangupa itself collects no personal information and has no account system or login. Your level progress and settings (sound, control scheme, directional pad, and similar preferences) are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases this information completely.

Advertising

Cangupa shows optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, that let you earn an extra hint by watching one to the end — never a condition to play the game itself. These ads are requested as non-personalized only: Cangupa does not use advertising identifiers to build a profile of you or track you across other apps or websites, and does not show its own App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Serving and measuring even a non-personalized ad still requires Google to process some technical data (such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general ad-interaction data) for ad delivery and fraud prevention. That processing is governed by Google's own policies, not Cangupa's:

  • Google AdMob's Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In-App Purchases

If Cangupa offers in-app purchases (for example, to unlock additional level packs), they are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Cangupa never sees or stores your payment details — only whether a purchase was completed, which is kept on your device the same way your progress is.

Children's Privacy

Cangupa is not directed at children and has no account creation or age-gating. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Cangupa doesn't send your progress or settings anywhere, there is nothing on our side to request the deletion of. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything stored on your device.

Your Rights

If you are in the European Union, the UK, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection law, you have rights over data that is processed about you — including access, correction, and erasure. Since Cangupa itself holds no personal data about you, these rights are best exercised directly with Google regarding the ad-serving data described above, using the links in the Advertising section.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted at this page, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@deficientezinho.pt.
