Sphinx is still in development — this page will turn into a real version-by-version log once it ships. For now, here's where things stand.

In progressbuilding toward a first release

Three modes, a growing word list, and the groundwork for a release

  • Guess It, Clear the List (Open Pool & Complete the Set), and Banned Words are all playable.
  • 24 categories across five languages, three age bands, and six color themes.
  • Custom categories, image mode, history, round photos, and alternate tilt/forehead controls.
A dated, per-version changelog starts with the first public release. Until then, this page is deliberately short rather than padded with internal build details.
