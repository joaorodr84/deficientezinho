Make it yours

Custom categories

Build your own category from scratch, with your own words — inside jokes, a birthday theme, a classroom unit. Created categories can be edited any time: rename them, add words, or retire the ones that didn't land.

Who's playing

Three age bands, one deck

Age bands are cumulative, not separate decks — picking an older band adds harder words on top of the easier ones, it never takes words away.

Band 1

Kids

The easiest tier in every category — every category guarantees at least 20 kid-friendly words.

Band 2

Teens

Kids' words plus a trickier middle tier.

Band 3

Adult

The full pool — kids' and teens' words plus the hardest tier.

Languages

Five languages, or follow your device

Every category and word is translated across all five — switch anytime in Settings, or let Sphinx pick based on your device's own language.

Color themes

Six palettes, light and dark

Pick a background and word color that suits the room — every theme's text and swipe colors are contrast-checked against WCAG AA so they stay readable across the board, not just the default.

And the rest

Everything else the app does today

  • Image modeSupported categories can show a picture instead of a word — handy for pre-readers or for Kids-band rounds.
  • Tilt gestures (experimental)Tilt the phone forward for Correct, backward for Pass — an alternative to tapping or swiping, no hands-free setup required.
  • Forehead modeHold the phone to your forehead instead of a table — motion sensors detect the pose and rotate the word so your team can read it.
  • Configurable round timerSet the clock to whatever length suits your group, per round.
  • HistoryEvery past round's category, score, and date, kept on-device until you clear it.
  • Round photoSnap a quick photo of the room after a round and save a framed copy — category, score and date included — to your photo library.
  • Play again, instantlyRestarts with the same category and age band — no re-navigating menus between rounds.
  • Everything stays on your deviceNo accounts, no ads, no analytics. The only thing ever sent off your phone is a crash report if something breaks. Read the privacy policy.
