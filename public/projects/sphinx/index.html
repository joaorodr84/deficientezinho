Sphinx

Describe it. Guess it. Never say it.

A party word game for one phone and a room full of people. Three ways to play, 24 categories, five languages, and a timer that's always running out.

How to play → See the game modes
How it works

One Mystery word at a time, against the clock

Sphinx's classic mode — "Guess It" — hands one player a secret word and a running timer. Everyone else has to guess it before time runs out, from nothing but a description that can't use the word itself.

01

Mystery appears

A word shows up on screen, picked from the category and age band you chose.

→
02

Describe it

One player explains it to the room without ever saying the word itself.

→
03

Swipe Correct or Pass

Got it? Swipe right. Stuck? Swipe left and move on — the clock doesn't stop.

→
04

Results

When the timer hits zero, see the score, swap describers, and go again.
