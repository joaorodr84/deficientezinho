Game modes

Three games, one app, the same room full of people

All three are pass-and-play on a single device — no second phone, no accounts, no internet required. Pick one from the Start dialog before a round; the choice is remembered like any other setting.

●Mode 1 of 3

Guess It

The original mode, and the one How to play covers in full. One player describes a Mystery word without saying it; everyone else guesses; swipe Correct or Pass against a running timer.

Umbrella
Works with every one of the 24 categories, in any age band, at whatever timer length you set.
  • PlayersA describer and everyone else, swapping roles each round.
  • ScoringOne point per Correct swipe. Passed words don't return this round.
  • Best forClassic charades-style party play, any group size.
●Mode 2 of 3

Clear the List

No typing, no voice recognition — just a checklist and a timer. One player (the Reviewer) holds the phone, which shows the real list of what to name. Everyone else calls out answers out loud, and the Reviewer taps each one off as it's correctly named.

Open Pool

The list is bigger than the goal — name twenty European countries out of the forty-plus that exist, for example. The Reviewer sees the entire pool and ticks anything named; the round ends at the target count or when time runs out.

Complete the Set

The list is the whole target — name all nine planets, say, with nothing extra in the pool. Every single item has to be ticked to clear it; the same checklist screen, just a tighter goal.

Easy Medium Hard

Every Clear the List category carries a difficulty tier, shown as the card's border color on the category picker — a quick read on how obscure the list gets before you commit to it.

  • PlayersOne Reviewer per round (device holder), one or more Players calling out guesses.
  • ScoringCorrectness is entirely the Reviewer's own judgment — there's no answer-matching to fool or be fooled by.
  • Best forGroups who'd rather shout names at each other than mime things.
●Mode 3 of 3

Banned Words

The same describe-and-guess loop as Guess It — swipe, timer and all — but the card also shows five or six words closely related to the Mystery word that the describer isn't allowed to say either. Describing "Pizza" without saying pizza is one challenge; doing it without "cheese," "slice," or "Italian" either is a very different one.

Pizza
Cheese Slice Italian Oven Round
Say the word, or any word on the strip, and the round's honor system — just like Correct and Pass already are — says you broke the rule.
  • PlayersSame as Guess It — a describer and everyone else, swapping each round.
  • ScoringIdentical to Guess It: swipe Correct or Pass, same timer, same card mechanic.
  • Best forGroups who've mastered Guess It and want the next challenge up.
