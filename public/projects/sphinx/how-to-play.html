How to play

The rules of Guess It, Sphinx's classic mode

This is the loop every round of the default game follows. Clear the List and Banned Words (see Game modes) bend a couple of these rules, but this is the one to learn first.

1Pick a category, an age band, and a timer

Before the round starts, choose what to play (one of the 24 built-in categories, or one you made yourself), who's playing (Kids, Teens, or Adult — each one includes every easier word below it, so nothing gets harder by picking an older band), and how long the clock runs. Then pass the phone to whoever's describing first.

2One word, one describer

A Mystery word appears on screen, visible only to the describer. Everyone else listens. The describer explains it however they like — synonyms, definitions, acting it out — with one rule: never say the word itself, or any close variant of it.

Umbrella
The describer sees the word. Nobody else does — they only hear the description.

3Swipe Correct or Pass

Someone in the room guesses it right — swipe the card right (or tap Correct) and the next word appears immediately. Nobody's getting it — swipe left (or tap Pass) to skip it and move on. Passing costs no time, but a passed word doesn't come back later in the same round, so it's a real trade-off, not a free skip.

4The clock doesn't wait

A timer runs the whole round, counting down from whatever length was set at the start. Words keep coming until it hits zero — there's no pausing to think, which is the whole point.

5Results, then go again

When time's up, the results screen shows the score and how many were Correct versus Passed. "Play again" restarts instantly with the same category and age band — pass the phone to the next describer and go. There's also an option to snap a quick photo of the room before moving on.
