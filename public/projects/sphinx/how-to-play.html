How to play

The rules of Guess It, Sphinx's classic mode

This is the loop every round of the default game follows. Clear the List and Banned Words (see Game modes) bend a couple of these rules, but this is the one to learn first.

1Choose what to play

Tap a category — or “Surprise me” for a random pick (Shuffle re-rolls it before you commit), or “All Categories” to draw from every category at once. The Start screen shows the category's cover art, its name, and three age bands — Kids, Teens, Adult — each cumulative: an older band adds harder words on top, it never takes the easy ones away. Pick a mode here too (Guess It is the default). Age band and mode are remembered the moment you pick them, not just on Start.

2Set your pace

Before or during a round, Settings lets you set the timer length, turn the timer off entirely for an untimed round, and cap how many times you're allowed to Pass — Unlimited, or 3, 5, 10. A capped round never strands you on a word nobody can get: Correct keeps working even once Pass is dimmed out.

3Describe it

A Mystery word appears, visible only to the describer. Everyone else listens — synonyms, acting it out, anything except the word itself or a close variant.

Umbrella
The describer sees the word. Nobody else does — they only hear the description.

4Answer however's comfortable

Swipe the card — right for Correct, left for Pass, with sensitivity adjustable per player in Settings — tap the on-screen buttons, or turn on Tilt gestures (forward for Correct, back for Pass, experimental) or Forehead mode, which turns the word to face outward while you hold the phone to your forehead. No single motion is required.

5The round ends one of three ways

Not just “time's up”: the clock hits zero, you hit a word cap if one's set, or — on a smaller category — the deck itself runs out. Each gets its own on-screen message before Results, so it never feels like the app just stopped.

Timer hits zero Word cap reached Deck runs out

6Results, pause, repeat

Results shows the score, Correct versus Passed, and the category. "Play again" restarts instantly with the same category and age band. Mid-round, the Round Menu (pause icon) lets you jump to Settings or leave the round without losing your place. There's also an optional prompt to snap a photo of the room after a round — framed with the category, score and date — which saves to your photo library, with a Retake option first.
