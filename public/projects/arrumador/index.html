Arrumador

Every crate has a place. Push it there.

You are the warehouse keeper. Push every crate onto a target in as few moves as you can, across 50 levels. Crates can be pushed but never pulled, so think before you shove.

Play in your browser →
How it works

One push at a time

Walk into a crate to push it one square. You can only push one crate at a time, and a crate shoved into a corner is stuck for good. The level is complete when every crate sits on a target.
