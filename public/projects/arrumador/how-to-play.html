How to play

Move every crate onto a target

Targets are marked on the floor. A crate turns green when it is on one.

  • 1Walk into a crate to push itIt moves one square in the direction you are walking.
  • 2One crate at a timeTwo crates in a row will not move.
  • 3No pullingCrates cannot be pulled, and they cannot pass through walls or other crates.
  • 4Watch the cornersA crate pushed into a corner is stuck for good. Undo or restart.
  • 5Fewer moves is betterThe game counts your moves and keeps your best for each level.
Controls

Keyboard or touch

  • MoveArrow keys or W A S D. On a phone, swipe on the board.
  • UndoZ, or the Desfazer button.
  • RestartR, or the Reiniciar button.
  • Pick a levelThe arrows step to the previous or next level, and Níveis opens the full list.
Saved in the browser

What it remembers

The level you were on and your best move count for each level. Nothing leaves your device.
