Damas

Checkers, the Portuguese way.

A checkers game that plays by Portuguese rules: flying kings, mandatory captures and the law of majority. Play on one screen, or against a friend over the internet with a six-character code.

Play in your browser →
How it works

The rules are enforced for you

The board only offers legal moves. When a capture is available you have to take it, and when several are, you take the one that captures the most pieces. Each rule can be switched off, and a free-play mode drops them all.
