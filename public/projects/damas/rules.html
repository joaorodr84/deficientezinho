Rules

Portuguese checkers

The default mode follows the Portuguese rule set on an 8×8 board.

  • Men move and capture forward onlyBackward capture by men is available as an option.
  • Kings flyA king moves any number of squares along a diagonal.
  • Captures are mandatoryIf you can capture, you must.
  • The law of majorityWhen more than one capture is possible, you must take the one that captures the most pieces.
  • Multi-captures are played square by squareThe captured pieces only leave the board when the whole move is complete.
Free play

Or play without a referee

Free-play mode lets you move pieces without the rule set, for house rules or for setting up a position.
