Online play

Two people, one code, no account

  • 1Create a gamePress Online, then Criar jogo. You get a six-character code and a link.
  • 2Send it to your friendCopy the code or the link and send it however you like.
  • 3They joinThey type the code and press Entrar, or just open the link.
  • 4PlayThe host plays white first. Colours swap on every new game, and the score is kept for the session.
The connection

Directly between the two browsers

Online play is peer-to-peer, over WebRTC through PeerJS. The public PeerJS broker is only used to introduce the two browsers; the moves go directly between them.

  • If the connection dropsThe guest reconnects automatically and the game resumes.
  • On strict networksA direct connection may not be possible on some networks.
