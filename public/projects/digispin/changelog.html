1.0.0

Submitted for App Store review

  • Submitted 2026-10-19. Not live on the App Store yet.
2026-10-17

Daily puzzle, five languages, Mastered stars

  • A daily puzzle, the same for every player each day, with a streak counter.
  • DigiSpin is now available in Portuguese, Spanish, French and German, alongside English — pick one in Settings, or follow the device.
  • A level is "Mastered" once every one of its solutions has been found; a pack is Mastered once every level in it is.
  • Fixed a bug where the app could get stuck on a blank screen if it couldn't read its saved data.
2026-10-16

A first-time tutorial, and ten level packs

  • A short interactive tutorial on first launch, and a "How to play" reference screen reachable from Settings at any time.
  • Levels are grouped into ten colour-named packs of 25. Every pack and every level can now be played in any order.
  • A new achievement, "Full house", for finding every solution in one pack.
2026-10-14

Two new quiet colour palettes

  • Dune and Dusk: two monochrome digit palettes, where every digit shares one colour and shape alone tells them apart.
  • Levels reordered by a refined, five-part difficulty score.
  • Hint now picks a random remaining piece instead of always the same one.
2026-10-10 to 2026-10-11

Redo, achievements, and accessibility passes

  • A Redo button alongside Undo and Restart.
  • Solve times are now recorded to the minute, and earned achievements show the date they were earned.
  • Screen reader announcements, contrast and touch-target fixes across the app.
Earlier

The foundations

  • The core game: drag pieces from the tray, rotate them, and place all ten with no two bars sharing a slot.
  • Light, dark and system themes, with a choice of digit colour palettes.
  • Hints, Undo, stats and an achievements system, saved on the device.
  • Replays: a solved level's other valid layouts unlock to view.
