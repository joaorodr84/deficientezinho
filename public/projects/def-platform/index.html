Um passeio do cara***

Roll through a city built by people who never tried it.

A 2D platformer where you roll a wheelchair through an obstacle course: curbs, ramps, a staircase-only "main entrance", a broken elevator, and shopping carts with strong opinions.

Play in your browser →
How it works

Momentum is the verb

Downhill ramps give free speed and uphill ramps eat it. The first gap can only be cleared with the speed from the hill before it. Hop the curbs, squash the carts, keep the battery charged and reach the finish gate.
