How to play

Controls

Touch devices get on-screen buttons.

KeyAction
← → / A D / numpad 4 6Roll
Space / ↑ / W / numpad 8 or 0Hop. A quick tap is a short hop; hold for a taller one.
numpad 5 / BBoost. Press once to turn it on, again to turn it off.
↓ / S / numpad 2Brake
P / R / M / OPause, restart, mute, settings
The level

How it works

  • MomentumDownhill ramps give free speed; uphill ramps eat it. Some gaps need the speed from the hill before them.
  • HopClears curbs. Small ledges are rolled over without hopping.
  • BoostRuns on a battery. Pick up a lightning bolt to refill it, which also heals you.
  • CartsShopping carts hurt on contact and cost three battery cells, but landing on one squashes it.
  • CheckpointsFlags bring you back after a fall, or when you run out of cells.
Stars

Three to earn

  • ★FinishReach the finish gate.
  • ★CollectPick up 80% of the tokens.
  • ★Beat parFinish under the level's par time.
Settings

Remembered in the browser

  • Boost modeToggle (the default) or hold.
  • LanguagePortuguês (Portugal), the default, or English.
  • CharacterThe hand-drawn Deficientezinho from the logo, or a classic wheelchair avatar.
