Accessibility

One key at a time

Platformers usually expect you to hold a direction and press jump together, often with a run button under a third finger. This one does not: nothing in it needs two keys at once.

  • Steering works in mid-airHop first, then tap a direction while you are in the air. You never hold a direction and press hop together.
  • Boost is a togglePress it once to turn it on and again to turn it off. Nothing to hold down. Hold mode is there in Settings for anyone who prefers it.
  • Hop height from one keyA quick tap is a short hop and a longer press is a taller one, so one key does both.
  • A whole control set on the numpadRoll, hop, boost and brake all sit on the numeric keypad, close together.
  • Touch buttonsOn a phone or tablet the same controls appear on screen.
